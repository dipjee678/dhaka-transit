      Transparent Provenance

      Every stop, route, timetable, and fare carries an explicit origin badge (Official, OpenStreetMap, Community, or Estimated). Missing bus routes or unverified fares are never fabricated.

      RAPTOR + Dijkstra

      Compare calendar-aware, Pareto-optimal RAPTOR schedules (distinguishing Sat–Thu from Friday afternoon MRT-6 service) side-by-side against frequency-based Dijkstra.

      Open Public Infrastructure

      Download the validated dhaka_gtfs.zip under ODbL 1.0, inspect our gap report, or submit a field-surveyed bus route directly from the Open Dataset page.
